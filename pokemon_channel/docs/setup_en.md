# Pokemon Channel Setup Guide

## You need

- [Archipelago](https://github.com/ArchipelagoMW/Archipelago/releases) 0.6.7 or newer
- [Dolphin](https://dolphin-emu.org/) and your own copy of Pokemon Channel (GPAE01, GPAP01 or GPAJ01)
- `pokemon_channel.apworld`

## Install

1. Double-click `pokemon_channel.apworld`, or put it in `Archipelago/custom_worlds`.
2. Open the Archipelago Launcher and pick **Generate Template Options** to get a `Pokemon Channel.yaml`.

## Play

1. Start a new save in Dolphin.
2. Open **Pokemon Channel Client** from the Launcher and connect to the room.
3. Before going to bed, check you have a Progressive Day for the next day. Before taking the bus, check you
   have the pass. With Channel Shuffle, only watch channels you've received.
4. Send checks with `/check <location name>` (part of the name is enough), list what's left with `/todo`,
   and send `/goal` when you finish.
